Privacy Policy

🙏 Jay Shri Radhe! Your privacy is sacred to us. Radhe Jap does not sell, share, or misuse your personal data in any way.

1. Introduction

Radhe Jap ("we", "our", or "the app") is a digital mala counter and spiritual companion app for Radha-Krishna devotees. This Privacy Policy explains how we handle your information when you use our app.

By using Radhe Jap, you agree to the practices described in this policy.

2. Information We Collect

Radhe Jap collects minimal information to provide its services:

  • Jap count data — your daily mala count, streaks, and sadhana records (stored locally on your device)
  • Sankalp / vow data — your personal spiritual goals (stored locally)
  • Account information — if you choose to sign in (name, phone number) for cloud backup only
  • App preferences — theme, language, and notification settings

3. How We Use Your Information

  • To save and display your jap count and spiritual progress
  • To send daily jap reminders (only if you enable notifications)
  • To back up your data to the cloud (only if you sign in)
  • To improve app performance and fix bugs

4. Data Storage

All your jap data is stored locally on your device by default. If you create an account, your data is securely backed up to our cloud servers to allow access across devices.

We do not store your data on third-party advertising servers.

5. We Do NOT

  • ❌ Sell your personal data to anyone
  • ❌ Show advertisements
  • ❌ Track your location
  • ❌ Access your contacts, camera, or microphone
  • ❌ Share your data with third parties for marketing

6. Third-Party Services

Radhe Jap may use the following trusted services:

  • Firebase (Google) — for authentication and cloud backup (optional)
  • Google Play Services — for app distribution and updates

These services have their own privacy policies. We encourage you to review them.

7. Children's Privacy

Radhe Jap is intended for users of all ages. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13. If you believe a child has provided us personal information, please contact us.

8. Data Security

We implement appropriate security measures to protect your spiritual data. Your jap counts and personal goals are private and not visible to other users.

9. Your Rights

  • You can delete your account and all associated data at any time from Settings
  • You can use the app completely offline without creating an account
  • You can request your data by contacting us
